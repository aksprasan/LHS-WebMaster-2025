What is TSA

Georgia TSA gives middle and high school students the opportunity to develop real world skills through hands-on competition and collaboration. With more than seventy competitive events available, members can pursue pathways that match their interests while still pushing themselves academically. Chapters often spend months designing, testing, and documenting their work, helping students learn how to think like engineers, communicate like professionals, and improve through feedback even when their first attempt is not perfect.

The experience culminates at the State Leadership Conference, typically held in March at the Classic Center in Athens. Thousands of students gather to present projects, take exams, interview with judges, and connect with other chapters, all while strengthening leadership through teamwork and chapter involvement.

Georgia TSA offers a wide range of events that allow every type of student to contribute, including:

  • Hands-on engineering challenges such as robotics, flight, and dragster style competitions
  • Technology and coding-based events including website design and digital problem solving
  • Communication-focused events like video production, promotional design, and prepared speaking

In addition to the State Leadership Conference, Georgia TSA hosts seasonal opportunities such as Technology Day at the Georgia National Fairgrounds in Perry, where students compete in additional contests, experience a high-energy rally atmosphere, and build confidence by performing under time pressure while learning from other competitors.

Who Are We

Lambert High School's TSA chapter is launching its first season with one goal: bringing the benefits of the Technology Student Association to more students in Forsyth County. Our founding officers recognized how TSA strengthens schools through leadership, teamwork, and real-world problem solving, and they worked to create a chapter that gives Lambert students a platform to innovate and compete.

Even though the approval process required careful planning and coordination with school administration, the officers stayed focused. By the start of the school year, Lambert TSA was officially established and introduced at the school's club fair, where it immediately gained interest and recruited a committed group of members.

In its first year, Lambert TSA is preparing for a full conference season, entering events across engineering, design, and communications. The chapter is focused on building a strong foundation by:

  • Training members and developing teamwork skills
  • Organizing regular team meetings
  • Creating projects that showcase Lambert's creativity and dedication

This season marks the beginning of a long-term tradition. Lambert TSA is proud to take its first steps toward making an impact locally, statewide, and beyond.

Our Events

Meetings

Attendance & Checkpoints

Before the start of every club meeting, each student must sign in to attendance via Google Form. Once a month, the officers send a checkpoint form through email to check up on the progress of each group. During the weekly after school meeting following this checkpoint, the officers meet with each group in person to check in on their progress, answer questions, and help them plan for the next week. The officers will advise each group as they move closer to the competition dates.

Weekly After School Meetings

To keep all students intact with their TSA event, the club runs weekly meetings to regroup the teams and update each other on any group progress. The club officers begin the meetings with recaps on competition dates, event updates, and general announcements. Throughout the meetings, students in each event regroup to share their personal progress on the project and to collaborate on plans for the upcoming competitions.

Recruitment

Lambert TSA actively recruits new members through school club fairs, information sessions, and word-of-mouth referrals. We welcome students from all grade levels and backgrounds who are interested in technology, engineering, and leadership development.

Our recruitment process includes:

  • Club fair presentations showcasing our projects and achievements
  • Information sessions explaining TSA events and opportunities
  • Mentorship programs pairing new members with experienced students
  • Open house events where prospective members can see our work firsthand

Involvement

Student involvement is at the heart of Lambert TSA. We encourage all members to actively participate in competitions, leadership roles, and chapter activities.

Ways students get involved:

  • Competing in individual and team events at state and national levels
  • Taking on officer positions and leadership responsibilities
  • Participating in community service and outreach programs
  • Attending conferences and professional development workshops

Community

Lambert TSA is committed to giving back to our local community and promoting STEM education among younger students.

Our community initiatives include:

  • Hosting STEM workshops for elementary and middle school students
  • Volunteering at local technology events and maker fairs
  • Partnering with local businesses and organizations
  • Organizing fundraising events to support chapter activities
