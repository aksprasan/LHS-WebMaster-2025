Career Pathways

Computer Science Pathway

The Computer Science pathway prepares students for careers in software development, IT, and technology innovation. Students learn programming fundamentals, cloud computing, and advanced software design principles. Through hands-on projects and real-world applications, students develop problem-solving skills and technical expertise needed for the modern tech industry.

Introduction to Software and Technology

This foundational CTAE course introduces students to a wide variety of digital technologies, including software, hardware, and emerging tools used in technology careers. Students learn programming basics, computational thinking, and problem-solving strategies. They gain hands-on experience with spreadsheets, word processing, and collaborative tools. By the end of the course, students are equipped with essential skills to pursue advanced computing and IT courses.

AP Computer Science Principles

AP Computer Science Principles introduces students to fundamental computing concepts, including algorithms, data, programming, and cybersecurity. Students explore how computing impacts society, business, and everyday life. Hands-on projects emphasize computational thinking, creativity, and collaboration. This course prepares students for future computer science studies and technology-driven careers.

AP Computer Science A

AP Computer Science A focuses on object-oriented programming in Java. Students design and implement complex programs while learning data structures, algorithms, and debugging strategies. The course emphasizes efficiency, problem solving, and code quality. Students gain a solid foundation for college-level CS and software development careers.

Cloud Computing

Cloud Computing introduces students to cloud architecture, service models, and deployment strategies. Students learn about storage solutions, virtualization, and collaborative tools. Practical exercises highlight security, scalability, and data management. This course prepares students for IT careers in modern cloud-based systems.

Business & Management Pathway

The Business & Management pathway equips students with essential business skills including financial literacy, accounting, and strategic management. Students learn to analyze markets, manage finances, and lead organizations. This pathway prepares students for careers in business administration, finance, and entrepreneurship.

Introduction to Business & Technology

This course provides an overview of essential business concepts and technology applications in professional settings. Students learn workplace communication, teamwork, and project management skills. Hands-on exercises reinforce technology use in business operations. By the end, students are prepared for careers in management, entrepreneurship, and business technology.

Financial Literacy

Financial Literacy teaches students how to manage money effectively, including budgeting, saving, and responsible spending. Students explore credit, investments, and long-term financial planning. Real-world examples develop decision-making and problem-solving skills. This course equips students to handle personal finances and prepare for future economic challenges.

Banking, Investments, and Insurance

Students explore banking operations, investments, and insurance principles. They analyze financial products, manage risk, and make informed decisions. Case studies highlight real-world financial applications. This course prepares students for careers in banking, investments, and financial management.

Principles of Accounting I

Principles of Accounting I teaches students to maintain accurate financial records for businesses. Students prepare balance sheets, income statements, and ledgers while learning ethical accounting practices. Emphasis is placed on analysis and critical thinking. This course lays the groundwork for careers in accounting, auditing, and business management.

Health Care Pathway

The Health Care pathway introduces students to diverse healthcare careers and medical sciences. Students learn patient care, medical terminology, biotechnology, and wellness principles. This pathway prepares students for careers in nursing, allied health, biotechnology, and healthcare administration.

Introduction to Health Care (IHC)

Introduction to Health Care exposes students to a wide range of healthcare careers and foundational medical concepts. Students learn medical terminology, patient care, and safety protocols. The course emphasizes teamwork, professionalism, and healthcare ethics. Students are prepared for further study in allied health, biotechnology, or nursing pathways.

Essentials to Biotechnology

Essentials to Biotechnology introduces core biological concepts and laboratory techniques used in healthcare and biotech. Topics include cellular biology, genetics, molecular methods, and lab safety. Students conduct experiments, document results, and analyze data. This course provides a foundation for advanced biotechnology studies and lab-based careers.

Essentials to Healthcare

Essentials to Healthcare focuses on wellness, nutrition, anatomy, and physiology. Students explore preventive healthcare, patient care basics, and health literacy. Hands-on exercises reinforce practical knowledge and ethical considerations. The course prepares students for healthcare, fitness, and allied health pathways.

Biotechnology

Biotechnology deepens students' understanding of molecular biology, genetics, and laboratory techniques. Students perform experiments, analyze results, and solve real-world problems. Topics include DNA/RNA technologies, genetic engineering, and bioinformatics. This course prepares students for careers in research, clinical labs, and biotech industries.

Phlebotomy

Phlebotomy teaches safe blood collection and preparation for certification. Students learn anatomy, venipuncture, infection control, and patient communication. Hands-on practice develops clinical skills and attention to detail. Students are prepared for healthcare support roles and laboratory careers.

Sports Medicine

Sports Medicine focuses on injury prevention, assessment, and treatment in athletic settings. Students learn anatomy, therapeutic techniques, and rehabilitation strategies. Practical exercises simulate real-world sports care scenarios. The course prepares students for careers in physical therapy, athletic training, and sports healthcare.

Engineering Pathway

The Engineering pathway develops students' technical skills in design, problem-solving, and innovation. Students learn engineering principles, CAD software, and hands-on project development. This pathway prepares students for careers in mechanical, civil, electrical, and software engineering.

Foundations of Engineering

Foundations of Engineering introduces engineering design, problem-solving, and technology applications. Students explore mechanics, materials, electronics, and CAD software. Hands-on projects emphasize creativity, collaboration, and critical thinking. This course prepares students for advanced STEM courses and engineering careers.

Engineering Concepts

Engineering Concepts builds on foundational knowledge through applied projects and problem solving. Students design, test, and refine solutions using engineering tools. Teamwork, project management, and iterative design are emphasized. The course prepares students for higher-level engineering studies and real-world STEM careers.

Engineering Applications

Engineering Applications allows students to apply principles to real-world challenges. Students work on interdisciplinary projects, analyze data, and optimize solutions. Innovation, sustainability, and professional practices are emphasized. The course prepares students for post-secondary education and careers in engineering and technology.

AV Technology & Film Pathway

The AV Technology & Film pathway develops students' creative and technical skills in media production. Students learn camera operation, audio engineering, editing, and storytelling. This pathway prepares students for careers in film, television, digital media, and content creation.

AV Technology & Film I

AV Technology & Film I introduces audio and video production, including camera operation, lighting, and sound recording. Students learn storytelling techniques and basic editing skills. Hands-on projects develop technical skills and creativity. The course prepares students for advanced media courses and digital media careers.

AV Technology & Film II

AV Technology & Film II expands media production skills with advanced editing, lighting, sound design, and special effects. Students work on collaborative projects simulating professional environments. Portfolio development and project management are emphasized. The course prepares students for media careers and higher education in film or digital media.

AV Technology & Film III

AV Technology & Film III emphasizes advanced media production, including cinematography, audio mastering, and professional project management. Students produce independent and group projects for portfolios. Creativity, technical expertise, and real-world application are highlighted. The course prepares students for post-secondary studies and media industry careers.

Marketing Pathway

The Marketing pathway teaches students to develop and execute marketing strategies for businesses and brands. Students learn consumer behavior, market research, digital marketing, and entrepreneurship. This pathway prepares students for careers in marketing, advertising, sales, and business management.

Marketing Principles

Marketing Principles introduces core concepts such as consumer behavior, market research, branding, and promotion. Students explore product development, advertising, and sales strategies. Hands-on examples demonstrate real-world marketing applications. The course provides foundational skills for marketing, business, and entrepreneurship careers.

Marketing and Entrepreneurship

Marketing and Entrepreneurship explores marketing strategies for new ventures. Students develop business plans, branding, and sales campaigns. Projects emphasize creativity, problem-solving, and practical application. The course prepares students for entrepreneurial careers and advanced business studies.

Introduction to Sports & Entertainment Marketing

This course focuses on marketing within sports, entertainment, and events industries. Students learn about sponsorships, promotions, fan engagement, and social media strategies. Hands-on projects simulate real-world marketing campaigns. The course prepares students for careers in sports marketing, entertainment, and event management.

Marketing Management

Marketing Management teaches students to plan, implement, and evaluate comprehensive marketing strategies. Students analyze market trends, create campaigns, and study consumer behavior. Leadership, strategy, and communication are emphasized. The course prepares students for professional marketing roles and advanced business studies.

Advanced Sports & Entertainment Marketing

Advanced Sports & Entertainment Marketing engages students in designing and executing large-scale marketing campaigns. Students analyze data, create brand strategies, and evaluate results. Creativity, collaboration, and strategic thinking are emphasized. The course prepares students for professional careers in sports, entertainment, and event marketing.
