CTAE Pathways

Computer Science Pathway

Description

The Computer Science pathway prepares students for careers in software development, IT, and technology innovation. Students learn programming fundamentals, cloud computing, and advanced software design principles. Through hands-on projects and real-world applications, students develop problem-solving skills and technical expertise needed for the modern tech industry.

Courses

Introduction to Software and Technology - Foundational CTAE course introducing digital technologies including software, hardware, and emerging tools. Students learn programming basics, computational thinking, and problem-solving strategies with hands-on experience in spreadsheets, word processing, and collaborative tools.

AP Computer Science Principles - Introduces fundamental computing concepts including algorithms, data, programming, and cybersecurity. Students explore computing's impact on society, business, and everyday life through hands-on projects emphasizing computational thinking, creativity, and collaboration.

AP Computer Science A - Focuses on object-oriented programming in Java. Students design and implement complex programs while learning data structures, algorithms, and debugging strategies. Emphasizes efficiency, problem solving, and code quality for college-level CS and software development careers.

Cloud Computing - Introduces cloud architecture, service models, and deployment strategies. Students learn storage solutions, virtualization, and collaborative tools. Practical exercises highlight security, scalability, and data management for IT careers in modern cloud-based systems.

Business & Management Pathway

Description

The Business & Management pathway equips students with essential business skills including financial literacy, accounting, and strategic management. Students learn to analyze markets, manage finances, and lead organizations. This pathway prepares students for careers in business administration, finance, and entrepreneurship.

Courses

Introduction to Business & Technology - Overview of essential business concepts and technology applications in professional settings. Students learn workplace communication, teamwork, and project management skills with hands-on exercises reinforcing technology use in business operations.

Financial Literacy - Teaches students how to manage money effectively including budgeting, saving, and responsible spending. Students explore credit, investments, and long-term financial planning with real-world examples developing decision-making and problem-solving skills.

Banking, Investments, and Insurance - Explores banking operations, investments, and insurance principles. Students analyze financial products, manage risk, and make informed decisions through case studies highlighting real-world financial applications.

Principles of Accounting I - Teaches students to maintain accurate financial records for businesses. Students prepare balance sheets, income statements, and ledgers while learning ethical accounting practices with emphasis on analysis and critical thinking.

Health Care Pathway

Description

The Health Care pathway introduces students to diverse healthcare careers and medical sciences. Students learn patient care, medical terminology, biotechnology, and wellness principles. This pathway prepares students for careers in nursing, allied health, biotechnology, and healthcare administration.

Courses

Introduction to Health Care (IHC) - Exposes students to a wide range of healthcare careers and foundational medical concepts. Students learn medical terminology, patient care, and safety protocols with emphasis on teamwork, professionalism, and healthcare ethics.

Essentials to Biotechnology - Introduces core biological concepts and laboratory techniques used in healthcare and biotech. Topics include cellular biology, genetics, molecular methods, and lab safety. Students conduct experiments, document results, and analyze data.

Essentials to Healthcare - Focuses on wellness, nutrition, anatomy, and physiology. Students explore preventive healthcare, patient care basics, and health literacy with hands-on exercises reinforcing practical knowledge and ethical considerations.

Biotechnology - Deepens understanding of molecular biology, genetics, and laboratory techniques. Students perform experiments, analyze results, and solve real-world problems including DNA/RNA technologies, genetic engineering, and bioinformatics.

Phlebotomy - Teaches safe blood collection and preparation for certification. Students learn anatomy, venipuncture, infection control, and patient communication with hands-on practice developing clinical skills and attention to detail.

Sports Medicine - Focuses on injury prevention, assessment, and treatment in athletic settings. Students learn anatomy, therapeutic techniques, and rehabilitation strategies with practical exercises simulating real-world sports care scenarios.

Engineering Pathway

Description

The Engineering pathway develops students' technical skills in design, problem-solving, and innovation. Students learn engineering principles, CAD software, and hands-on project development. This pathway prepares students for careers in mechanical, civil, electrical, and software engineering.

Courses

Foundations of Engineering - Introduces engineering design, problem-solving, and technology applications. Students explore mechanics, materials, electronics, and CAD software with hands-on projects emphasizing creativity, collaboration, and critical thinking.

Engineering Concepts - Builds on foundational knowledge through applied projects and problem solving. Students design, test, and refine solutions using engineering tools with emphasis on teamwork, project management, and iterative design.

Engineering Applications - Allows students to apply principles to real-world challenges. Students work on interdisciplinary projects, analyze data, and optimize solutions with emphasis on innovation, sustainability, and professional practices.

AV Technology & Film Pathway

Description

The AV Technology & Film pathway develops students' creative and technical skills in media production. Students learn camera operation, audio engineering, editing, and storytelling. This pathway prepares students for careers in film, television, digital media, and content creation.

Courses

AV Technology & Film I - Introduces audio and video production including camera operation, lighting, and sound recording. Students learn storytelling techniques and basic editing skills with hands-on projects developing technical skills and creativity.

AV Technology & Film II - Expands media production skills with advanced editing, lighting, sound design, and special effects. Students work on collaborative projects simulating professional environments with emphasis on portfolio development and project management.

AV Technology & Film III - Emphasizes advanced media production including cinematography, audio mastering, and professional project management. Students produce independent and group projects for portfolios with focus on creativity, technical expertise, and real-world application.

Marketing Pathway

Description

The Marketing pathway teaches students to develop and execute marketing strategies for businesses and brands. Students learn consumer behavior, market research, digital marketing, and entrepreneurship. This pathway prepares students for careers in marketing, advertising, sales, and business management.

Courses

Marketing Principles - Introduces core concepts such as consumer behavior, market research, branding, and promotion. Students explore product development, advertising, and sales strategies with hands-on examples demonstrating real-world marketing applications.

Marketing and Entrepreneurship - Explores marketing strategies for new ventures. Students develop business plans, branding, and sales campaigns with projects emphasizing creativity, problem-solving, and practical application.

Introduction to Sports & Entertainment Marketing - Focuses on marketing within sports, entertainment, and events industries. Students learn about sponsorships, promotions, fan engagement, and social media strategies with hands-on projects simulating real-world marketing campaigns.

Marketing Management - Teaches students to plan, implement, and evaluate comprehensive marketing strategies. Students analyze market trends, create campaigns, and study consumer behavior with emphasis on leadership, strategy, and communication.

Advanced Sports & Entertainment Marketing - Engages students in designing and executing large-scale marketing campaigns. Students analyze data, create brand strategies, and evaluate results with emphasis on creativity, collaboration, and strategic thinking.
